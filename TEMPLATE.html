What would this page have to say to get a green flag at layer 3?

Everything above is a rewrite of material you already have. The harder question is what this resume should be arguing in the first place — and that depends on the role you are aiming at, which this review does not know.

Mapping the page to the role you actually want The same experience makes a different case depending on the job description you set beside it. Which of your projects argue for the role you are targeting, which ones are taking up space, and what is missing that the target role screens for?
The thought process behind a green flag What does a technical manager need to see before they mark a line "worth an interview"? Less about wording than about what you chose to put there — which problem you led with, how much difficulty you exposed, and what you left out.
What happens after layer 3 says yes Clearing the screen gets you into the room, not through it. The questions a panel opens with, how they probe the claims your resume made, and what decides the outcome once the document has done its job.

If you want to work through these against your target role and your actual resume, that is what a session is for.
